Privacy policy: career-ops (personal use)

This app is used by a single person, its owner, on their own Google account.

Data accessed: read-only access (gmail.readonly) to the emails filed under one Gmail label ("Job Leads"), to extract links to job postings.

Where data goes: job links are stored locally on the owner's computer. No email content is sold or shared. Job links may be processed by the AI assistant the owner uses to review postings.

Control: the owner can delete the local data at any time and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
